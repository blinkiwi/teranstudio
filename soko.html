SOKO

Info

→ SOKO is a 21st-century, old-school butcher shop and deli that focuses on sourcing from local and sustainable farms that raise their animals ethically and humanely.

Inspired by early 1900s butcher shops, our goal was to underscore the value of shopping at a local neighborhood butchery rather than at a chain grocery store. We have created a simple and elegant wordmark using a customized antique wood typeface. The accompanying visual identity includes a custom badge, an ‘S’ monogram, antique animal illustrations, hand-gilded gold window lettering, and a classic illuminated blade sign.
