Lost Sock Roasters

Info

→ Lost Sock Roasters is a small batch specialty coffee roastery and cafe located in Washington, DC. Just like a lost sock, a good cup of coffee is something you look for when you wake up in the morning.

We focused on what made their name and concept unique. Inspired by retro tube socks, the design language of the lost sock shows up in subtle, unexpected ways. A sock is not present in the logo. Instead, playful stripes appear on the coffee cups and packaging giving the customer a moment of discovery when they make the connection.
