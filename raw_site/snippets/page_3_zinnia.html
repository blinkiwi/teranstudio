→ Mrs. K’s Toll House was a local institution in Silver Spring, Maryland. Starting as an actual toll house along a main thoroughfare out of Washington, DC over 150 years ago, it was turned into a restaurant in the 1930s. When Mrs. K’s closed in 2020, a new multi-restaurant concept, Zinnia, took its place.

Inspired by its rich history and beautiful gardens, the new spot includes a cafe, tavern, fine-dining restaurant, and outdoor garden, each offering a different dining experience. In crafting the new identity, we curated a collection of vintage and modern zinnia illustrations along with antique photographs, and postcards from the Mrs. K’s era, making this spot feel new yet familiar at the same time.
