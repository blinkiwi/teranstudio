Motorkat

→ Back in the 1990’s, a thrill-seeking feline named Motor Cat cruised the streets on the front of her owner’s Suzuki 500 motorcycle. She quickly became an emblem of the progressive quirky nature of Takoma Park, Maryland. 30 years later, we celebrate this local legend at Motorkat, a restaurant centered around a seafood raw bar and wood-fired dishes by chef Danny Wells.

The identity pays homage to vintage motorcycles and an industrial aesthetic. The menu is reminiscent of vintage sign lettering from a bygone era, while hand-painted windows and a glowing neon sign speak to a fun and eclectic neighborhood haunt.
