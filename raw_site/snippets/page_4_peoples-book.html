→ People’s Book is an independent bookstore in Takoma Park, Maryland. The name references the town’s nickname, ‘The People’s Republic of Takoma Park,’ and is also inspired by ‘People’s Drug,’ a local Washington, DC drugstore chain from the past.

Inspired by the store’s tagline ‘You Belong,’ we created a set of ‘people’ to represent the name, diversity, and activist roots of the neighborhood. The choice of yellow as the main color, warm and optimistic, helps People’s Book stand out on Main Street and distinguish itself from other bookstores in the region.
