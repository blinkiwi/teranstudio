→ Indigro is a boutique plant shop that offers houseplants and plant design services. We crafted an identity to stand out in the Washington DC plant shop market, one that is fun and vibrant, and reflects its woman-owned status. A heart-shaped Monstera leaf symbolizes the love and care the owner invests in every plant and customer. The use of pink highlights the unique variegated leaves found in certain plants. We also printed custom letterpress tags to provide owners with a straightforward guide on how to care for their new plants.
