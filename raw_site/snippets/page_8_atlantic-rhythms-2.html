→ The design for Atlantic Rhythms’
“Miru Mira” by Shayna Dunkelman & Javier Areal Vélez takes inspiration from both the music and Japanese woodcut art shared by the musicians. Through custom die-cutting, the packaging folds to reveal a complex, layered pattern, offering glimpses of the blue cassette inside. This approach not only visually represents the collaborative and experimental process behind the album but also elevates the J-card and cassette into a sculptural artwork, blending form with function.
