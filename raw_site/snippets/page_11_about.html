Our Services

Have an idea, not a plan.

No Plan is a full-service graphic design, letterpress, and art studio located in the vibrant community of Takoma Park, Maryland just outside of Washington, DC. We are guided by the idea that the creative process isn’t a straight path, and that embracing unpredictability can lead to fresh perspectives. We push boundaries by exploring new ways to approach everyday subjects. Inspired by the juxtaposition of new and old technologies, our work combines the latest in modern design with century-old vintage equipment and craft.

We’re hyperlocal—we know investing in our neighbors is investing in our community. We value collaborative partnerships with our clients, as we work closely to understand their unique needs and vision. Led by the husband and wife team of Jesse and Dianne Kirsch, both accomplished graphic designers and artists, we provide a dedicated and hands-on experience throughout the entire process. You’re not just hiring us; you’re partnering with us.

Jesse Kirsch is a multi-talented creative, working as a graphic designer, muralist, letterpress printer, and artist. With a profound appreciation for the craftsmanship and creativity of bygone eras, Jesse values the artistry and dedication that characterized creative professions in the past. His endeavors have led to the creation of authentic brands and designs, leaving a memorable mark on the visual landscape. Jesse earned his BFA in Graphic Design from the School of Visual Arts in New York City.

Dianne Kirsch is an experienced graphic designer with a passion for illustration. With over two decades of experience specializing in brand identity, art direction, print collateral, books, and annual reports, Dianne brings a unique and creative approach to her work. Her diverse experience includes Fortune 500 companies, nonprofits, and startups. She holds a BFA in Graphic Design from Washington University in St. Louis.

At the heart of our work lies a genuine love for community, design, and authentic experiences.
