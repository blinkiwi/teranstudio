No Way

ABCDC

→ The mural, ‘No Way,’ subverts traditional wayfinding markers with a bold grid of graphic arrows that captures the energy of the built environment. The arrows serve as a metaphor for the choices we make in our lives—we can follow the path that is laid out for us or we can forge our own way. The playful nods to rebellion create a thought-provoking work that encourages viewers to reimagine their relationship with the world and embrace their own sense of adventure.

Client: NoMa BID
